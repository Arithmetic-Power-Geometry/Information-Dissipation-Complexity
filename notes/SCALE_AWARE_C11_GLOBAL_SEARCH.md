# Scale-aware global C=11 search

## Purpose

Combine the strongest four-level upper-bound geometry with the physical-scale correction discovered during the five-root search.

The earlier chamber search used (x=(0,1,u,v)). That is valid for the all-real-zero upper-bound analysis, but incomplete for physical-root discovery on (t>1). The corrected search uses
[
x=a(0,1,u,v),qquad a>0.
]

## Domain

Only the four generic chambers with coefficient sign variation
[
mathcal C=11
]
are searched. Their membership is enforced by the ten affine wall signs.

For each chamber the search varies:
- the absolute scale (a);
- the two normalized geometry parameters (u,v);
- three independent positive multiplicity ratios.

The objective is the actual number of sign-changing zeros of (kappa_3(t)) on (1<tle1500), not coefficient sign variation.

## Promotion rule

Any result above five is a discovery candidate only. It must survive:
1. high-precision root refinement;
2. rational information-gap approximation;
3. integer multiplicity approximation;
4. high-precision recount;
5. outward-rounded interval sign certificates on disjoint physical brackets.

Until that chain succeeds, the theorem remains
[
5le N_{max}(4)le11.
]
