# Four-level coefficient-dependency audit

## Goal

The current four-level upper bound
[
N_V(W)le 11
]
comes from rate-ordered sign variation of the exponential-polynomial numerator of (kappa_3). The coefficients, however, are not independent: all pair and triple terms are generated by four information levels and four positive tilted-mass prefactors.

The purpose of this stage is to identify algebraic constraints that may sharpen the variation bound. No sharper zero bound is claimed here unless the constraints are coupled to a valid zero-count theorem.

## Important limitation

These dependencies by themselves do **not** reduce the generalized-Descartes bound. A sequence may satisfy strong magnitude constraints and still retain the same rate-ordered sign variation. To prove (N_Vle 7), (N_Vle5), or another sharper ceiling, one needs an additional theorem connecting these compatibility relations to zeros—for example through total positivity, Hankel/minor constraints, or a suitable variation-diminishing representation.

## Next analytic tests

1. Express the rate-ordered coefficient vector in each of the four (mathcal C=11) chambers as a six-parameter positive monomial map.
2. Compute low-order Hankel and adjacent minors symbolically after factoring positive monomials.
3. Determine whether their signs are geometry-forced within a chamber.
4. If a fixed-sign minor pattern exists, test whether a known total-positivity/extended-Chebyshev theorem reduces the number of zeros.
5. Separately test whether (kappa_3) can have six distinct physical zeros by solving the six interpolation equations directly; failure is only evidence, while an algebraic incompatibility certificate could become a theorem.

This is the transition from computational root discovery to an analytic obstruction program.
